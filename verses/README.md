# Open verse: Somebody's …

Verse 3 of the song is a roll call. Each line names a job somebody is doing so that things go well, and a crowd answers *"Somebody!"* Add yours.

## The form

Two lines that rhyme, each starting with **Somebody's**, each about **eleven syllables or fewer** so it sings at 128 BPM:

```
Somebody's testing it before it flies, (Somebody!)
Somebody's teaching the kids to ask why, (Somebody!)
```

The video draws one paper tile per line. The last tile of the verse is left empty and says **you**.

## What makes a good line

* A real job that helps things go well, either a **guardrail** (testing, rules, watching, honesty) or a **garden** (teaching, curing, feeding, mending). The best pairs have one of each.
* Concrete beats abstract. "Checking the brakes" beats "ensuring safety."
* Honest beats triumphant. Nothing that promises the future is settled, either way.

## What gets sent back

* Real people, companies, brands or politicians
* Anything that mocks people who work on AI risk, or people who don't
* Anything that claims the danger is over, or that it can't be helped
* Lyrics copied from anywhere else

## How to add yours

1. Copy `_template.md` to `verses/your-handle.md`.
2. Fill in two to six lines, plus a one-line description of the picture for each tile.
3. Open a pull request. Say whether you used an AI tool to write the lines. Either is welcome; say which.

The best ones may be sung in a second take and drawn for a follow-up.
